inTense: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

inTense (“the app”) is a Spanish grammar trainer made by Daria Naidina, an individual developer.

What the app collects

Nothing leaves your iPhone. The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect your name, e-mail, contacts, location or any identifier.

Everything the app stores stays on your iPhone:

  • your learning progress (topics, answers, mistakes to review, days in a row, placement result);
  • your settings (languages, theme, accent check, session length, reminder time);
  • your subscription status (plan, end date, whether it renews), so Pro keeps working offline.

If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or to a computer, iOS includes this data in your backup, as it does for every app. That backup is yours and is handled by Apple or your computer; the app itself sends nothing anywhere.

Purchases

inTense Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The app asks Apple’s App Store on your iPhone (StoreKit) whether your Apple Account has an active subscription and receives only that answer (plan, end date, whether it renews); it never sees your payment details or Apple Account information.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and shares no data with anyone. If App Store Connect’s optional app analytics is on for your device, Apple collects it under its own policy, only if you agreed to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your iPhone. No notification service or server is involved.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your iPhone until you delete it. To delete everything, delete the app: iOS removes all of its data. There is nothing stored elsewhere to ask us to delete. To cancel a subscription, use Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
