inTense: Grammar Trainer for iPhone

inTense teaches Spanish verb tenses as a system, from A1 to C1. A short placement test finds your level; then each tense has three steps: Understand (the rule and the tables), Practise (verb forms) and Apply (forms in real sentences). Your mistakes come back for review. Everything stays on your iPhone: no account, no tracking.

Contact

Questions, a mistake in a sentence, or a problem with the app: write to intensegrammar@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something doesn’t work. Every message is read and answered.

Restore your purchase

If you bought inTense Pro and the app shows Free (for example on a new iPhone or after reinstalling):

  • Open inTense and go to Settings › Restore purchases, or tap Restore purchases at the bottom of the inTense Pro offer screen.
  • Use the same Apple Account that bought the subscription, and make sure you’re online.

Manage or cancel the subscription

inTense Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold by Apple. You manage it in your iPhone’s settings, not in the app:

  • Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose inTense. While Pro is active, Settings › Manage subscription in inTense takes you there too.
  • The 7-day free trial is on the yearly plan only. It becomes a paid subscription when the trial ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before.
  • When Pro ends, nothing is deleted: your progress stays, and the Pro features lock.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Your progress is stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it. Details: Privacy Policy.
